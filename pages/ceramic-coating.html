Why get a ceramic coating?×

Four reasons. Long-term protection for the paint. Easier washing. It stays cleaner longer. And it sheds dirt and water on its own, so the vehicle looks washed for longer between washes.

Which package is mine?×

If you drive it every day, Everyday Protection.

If it's the one you'd never let a car wash touch, Pride Car Protection.

Either way the paint inspection sets the correction, so you're never paying for work the paint doesn't need.

Does it need a detail first?×

Every coating starts with a wash and clay. Anything bonded to the paint gets pulled off so the coating bonds to the paint itself. That prep is part of the quote.

Does it make the paint scratch-proof?×

No. It's a hard layer, not armor. It takes the swirls a wash would put in, shrugs off sun and bird droppings, and makes everything easier to clean. Rock chips still win.

How much is it?×

Quoted on your vehicle, in person. The paint's condition sets most of the price, and that can't be read from a photo.

You get one number before it's scheduled. Nothing gets added on the day.

Do I need to be there?×

For the inspection, yes. We walk the paint with you and agree on the package before anything is touched.
