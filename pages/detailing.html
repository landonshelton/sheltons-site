Condition Guide

Pick the level that sounds closest to your vehicle. All vehicles are inspected on site before the detail starts.

Level 0

Maintenance level

Exterior

Light dust to moderate dirt. Minimal brake dust. No bugs, tar, overspray, etching, or water spots. No scratches or paint issues. Trim, headlights, and engine in like-new condition.

Interior

Minimal dust only. No trash, stains, spills, odors, pet hair, food debris, sticky residue, or biohazards. Carpets lightly dusted.

Level 1

Average detail level

Exterior

Mild to moderate dirt and brake dust. Light bug splatter. No mud, tar, overspray, water spots, etching, or scratches. Paint smooth, no clay needed. Trim, headlights, and engine in like-new condition.

Interior

Mild to moderate dust and light debris. Carpets mildly dirty, no extraction. No stains, odors, pet hair, food debris, sticky residue, or biohazards.

Level 2

Mini reset

Exterior

Mild to moderately dirty, moderate to heavy brake dust, possible bug splatter. Light paint roughness, clay included. Trim like new. Minimal swirl marks, paint enhancement included. No scratches, tar, overspray, water spots, or other paint issues. Headlights and engine like new.

Interior

Mild to moderate dust and light debris. Carpets mildly dirty, vacuum only. No stains, food or sticky residue, pet hair, odors, or biohazards.

Level 3

Full restoration

Exterior

Moderate to extreme dirt, brake dust, and bug buildup. Paint roughness with moderate to heavy swirl marks, scratches, tar or overspray, etching, and water spots. Unique paint concerns present. Trim, headlights, and engine faded or oxidised and need restoration.

Interior

Heavy dust, debris, carpet dirt, stains, food residue, pet hair, and strong odors such as smoke or mold. Headliner heavily stained. Biohazard present.

Which level am I?×

Pick the closest level on the Condition Guide. Most daily drivers are a Level 1.

Kids, dogs, a lake summer or a year without a wash is usually a Level 2, the Mini Reset.

Scratched, etched or faded paint with stains and smells inside is a Level 3, a Restoration.

Level 2 and Level 3 get a quote through our booking window before anything is scheduled.

Pick wrong and nothing happens. Every vehicle is re-inspected in your driveway with you before we start.

How long does it take?×

Three to six hours on the vehicle, depending on size and level. You get the time window when we confirm.

Do I need to be home?×

For the start, yes. We walk the vehicle with you and re-inspect it against the Condition Guide before anything is touched.

After that, go about your day. We need a water spigot and an outlet.

What's the difference between this and a car wash?×

A wash gets the loose dirt off and puts swirls in your paint doing it.

A detail starts with a foam pre-wash and a two-bucket hand wash, then clay and iron to pull out what's bonded to the paint, then a sealant so it stays slick for months. Inside, every panel, vent, seam and seat gets cleaned, not wiped.

It's three to six hours on the vehicle for a reason.

How much is it?×

A Full Detail is $300 for a coupe or sedan, $330 for a small or mid SUV, $370 for a truck or large SUV.

The Premium Interior Detail and the Wash, Clay and Seal cost less. Every price shows in the booking window before you commit.

The Mini Reset and Restoration are quoted through the booking window, then confirmed in your driveway before we start. Nothing gets added on the day.

What if I'm not happy with it?×

Call or text within 48 hours and we come back and make it right, no charge and no argument. If you're not happy, we aren't happy.
