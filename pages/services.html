FAQ

I don't know what my vehicle needs. Where do I start?

Detailing, nine times out of ten. The vehicle rating guide on that page lets you pick the condition it's in and shows what that detail covers. If it's already clean and you want it kept that way, that's Maintenance.

What's the difference between a detail and maintenance?

A detail is one visit that brings the vehicle back to our standard. Maintenance is our crew coming back every two weeks to keep it there. Most maintenance clients start with a detail.

Do I need to be home?

Just for the start. We walk the vehicle with you and re-inspect it before we touch anything. After that you can go about your day. All we need is a water spigot and an outlet.

Where do you go?

Cedar Creek Lake and Henderson County, at your driveway. Further out across East Texas by arrangement.
