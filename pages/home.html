FAQ

What is Shelton's Premium Detail?

A mobile detailing company on Cedar Creek Lake. We come to your driveway, use your water spigot and an outlet, and hand your vehicle back looking like the day you bought it. The highest-rated detailer on the lake, by a long way.

How do I schedule a detail?

Hit Book Now, choose your vehicle size and the service, then grab a day and time. We'll text you to confirm. On the day we show up when we said we would and walk the vehicle with you before we start. You don't pay a deposit, and you won't be playing phone tag with us.

Who is this for?

Owners who'd rather have it done right than done fast. New cars you want to keep new, lake cars that live in the sun, work trucks, busy lake moms who'd rather not clean up after the kids, Porsches, and the Corvette you only drive on Sundays.

How much does it cost?

A Full Detail starts at $300 for a coupe or sedan, $330 for a small or mid SUV, $370 for a truck or large SUV. Exterior-only and interior-only tiers run less. Every price shows in Book Now before you commit, and there's nothing added on the day.

What's the difference between a detail and a car wash?

A wash gets the loose dirt off and puts swirls in your paint doing it. A detail starts with a foam pre-wash and a two-bucket hand wash, then clay and iron to pull out what's bonded to the paint, then a sealant so it stays slick for months. Inside, we clean and condition every panel, vent, seam and seat instead of just wiping them down. That's why it takes three to six hours.

What if I'm not happy with it?

Call or text us within 48 hours and we'll come back and fix it for free. We won't argue with you about it. We've got 85 five-star reviews, and only two people have ever needed that promise.
