What actually happens every two weeks?×

A thorough maintenance detail, inside and out, that brings your vehicle back to our professional standard.

Think of it as a reset at your driveway, done by a professional crew working from your vehicle's file.

That crew keeps notes on your vehicle's history with us: how many times it's been serviced, what was done, and whether it's due for something.

We also keep your preferences saved. How you like the center console organised. What scent you like in the car. How you want the vehicles arranged in the driveway or the garage.

You get a text when we start and a text when we're done.

Is this right for me?×

If you work a lot, this is for you. The vehicle gets handled while you're at the office.

If you travel a lot, it's clean when you land.

If you have kids, two weeks of a back seat is a different vehicle. You never have to see it.

If you're a busy entrepreneur who values your time but still wants it done professionally, this is for you.

If you keep your vehicle maintained because that's the standard you hold yourself to, you already know.

If a $25 wash is what you're after, this isn't it.

What if I travel?×

If a visit lands while you're away, the card on file is still charged for that visit.

You keep that visit. We credit it to another maintenance visit or to any other service we offer.

If you travel regularly, or run more than one vehicle (one stays home for work, one is the daily), that's what our custom maintenance plan is for.

We put together a schedule and a set of services that fit your calendar.

What if I pay for the year in advance?×

Then you've bought 23 full maintenance visits and 3 full mini resets. Twenty-six visits a year, every other week.

Miss a visit? It rolls over. We don't charge you again until every visit you paid for has been completed.

You also get 10% off the top. Paying in advance tells us we have a committed client for the year, and it lets us lock your route, your crew and our product orders twelve months out. That saves us money, so we pass it to you.

How much is it?×

$300 a month per vehicle. That's $150 a visit, every other week. No contract.

To get on the plan, the vehicle is brought up to our standard first. That first visit is the Maintenance Start Kit, and its price is in the booking window above.

That price is what lets us:

1) put only our best techs on maintenance clients, not whoever's free that day

2) use the same professional-grade products and tools on your vehicle every visit

3) keep a file on your vehicle, every service and every preference, so we never have to ask you twice

4) re-inspect it every four months and tell you what's changed before it becomes a problem

5) keep our routes tight enough to show up on the day we said, at the time we said, every other week, all year

6) carry the 48-hour re-do: if a visit isn't right, we come back and make it right, no charge

A cheaper plan would have to cut one of those.

If a $25 wash is what you're after, don't do it.
